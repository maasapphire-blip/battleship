      How to Play Battleship (and How to Win)

      The Goal

      You and the AI each hide a fleet of 5 ships on a secret 10×10 grid. You take turns firing at each other's grid. The first player to sink all 5 enemy ships wins.

      Step 1 — Place Your Ships

      1. Ships are placed on Your Fleet (the left board).
      2. Click a square to place the current ship. Press R or Rotate to switch between horizontal and vertical.
      3. Ships must fit on the board and cannot overlap. They may touch each other.
      4. In a hurry? Click Random Placement. Then press Start Battle.

      Step 2 — Take Turns Firing

      1. Click any square on Enemy Waters (the right board) to fire a shot.
      2. 🔴 Red = Hit — part of a ship is there.
      3. ⚪ Grey dot = Miss — only water.
      4. When every square of a ship is hit, it is sunk and the game announces which ship went down.
      5. After your shot, the AI fires at your board. Watch your fleet on the left.
      6. You can't fire at the same square twice.

      How to Win — Strategy Tips for Beginners

      • Hunt, then target. Fire spread-out shots until you get a hit, then fire at the squares directly next to it (up, down, left, right) to find the rest of the ship.
      • Follow the line. Once you have two hits in a row, keep firing along that line until the ship sinks or you miss; then try the other end.
      • Use the checkerboard trick. Every ship is at least 2 squares long, so shooting only every other square (like the dark squares on a chessboard) still guarantees you'll touch every ship while using half the shots.
      • Don't forget the small ships. The 2-square Destroyer is the hardest to find — check the corners and edges near the end.
      • Count the enemy's remaining ships. The fleet list under the enemy board shows what's left, so you know how big a gap you're searching for.
      • Hide your own fleet wisely. Avoid placing all your ships in the centre or in a neat row; spreading them out — with some on the edges — makes the AI work harder.
